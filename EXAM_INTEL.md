## 1. Exam Structure

| Item | Detail (verified) |
|---|---|
| Total questions | **150** |
| Scored questions | **135** |
| Pretest (unscored) questions | **15**, randomly mixed in; used to validate future questions |
| Time limit | **3 hours (180 minutes)** (~72 sec/question) |
| Break | **One 10-minute break** after question 75; once you review answers and start the break, you CANNOT return to questions 1–75 |
| Tutorial + survey | Optional, 5–15 min, NOT counted in the 3 hours |
| Languages | English, Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Spanish, Korean, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional) |
| Question types | (1) **Multiple choice** (dominant format), (2) **Drag-and-drop / enhanced matching** (two columns, match left→right), (3) **Hot spot / point-and-click** (click a specific area of an image; marked with an X or highlighted), (4) **Animation video** (watch scenario, answer MCQ — has audio + closed captioning; test-center only), (5) **Comic strip** (read scenario, answer MCQ; used for online-proctored candidates instead of animations to avoid sound issues) |
| Notes | One question per animation/comic strip. Exact mix of question types varies by exam form. |

## 2. Exam Content Outline — Domains & Weightings (VERIFIED vs pmi.org ECO)

From the official **CAPM Examination Content Outline (2023 Exam Update)**:

| # | Domain | % of Items on Test |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Project Management Fundamentals and Core Concepts** | **36%** |
| II | **Predictive, Plan-Based Methodologies** | **17%** |
| III | **Agile Frameworks/Methodologies** | **20%** |
| IV | **Business Analysis Frameworks** | **27%** |
| | **Total** | **100%** |

Approx. scored-question counts (135 scored × weight, approximate): Fundamentals ≈ 49, Business Analysis ≈ 36, Agile ≈ 27, Predictive ≈ 23.

**Key tasks per domain (from the ECO):**

- **Domain I — Fundamentals (36%):** life cycles & processes (project vs program vs portfolio; issues vs risks vs assumptions vs constraints); planning (cost/quality/risk/schedule plans; milestone vs task duration; risk & stakeholder registers); roles & responsibilities (PM vs sponsor; leadership vs management; emotional intelligence); executing planned strategies/frameworks; problem-solving tools (meetings, focus groups, standups, brainstorming); **Code of Ethics applied to scenarios**.
- **Domain II — Predictive (17%):** when predictive fits (org structure: virtual, colocation, matrix, hierarchical); **critical path method**; **schedule variance**; **WBS & work packages**; quality & integration management plans; project controls/artifacts; **cost & schedule variance calculations (EVM-style)**.
- **Domain III — Agile (20%):** when adaptive fits (pros/cons vs predictive); planning iterations (logical units of iteration, WBS→iteration translation, adaptive tracking); adaptive artifacts; components of adaptive plans (**Scrum, XP, SAFe, Kanban**); task prioritization & success criteria in adaptive projects.
- **Domain IV — Business Analysis (27%):** BA roles (process owner/manager, product manager/owner; internal vs external); stakeholder communication (choosing channels/tools); **gathering requirements** (user stories vs use cases; interviews/surveys/workshops; **requirements traceability matrix / product backlog**); **product roadmaps** (components → releases); how methodology shapes BA work; validating requirements via acceptance criteria & delivery readiness.

PMI's note: predictive/adaptive/business-analysis approaches appear **throughout all four domains**, not isolated to "their" domain.

## 3. How CAPM Maps to the PMBOK Guide 7th Edition

**What the PMBOK 7 covers** (confirmed in local extract `/home/kg/capm-dojo/session/pmbok_full.txt`):
- **12 principles of project management** and **8 project performance domains** (a principles-based shift away from the 49-process/10-knowledge-area structure of PMBOK 6).
- A dedicated **Tailoring** section, plus expanded **tools, techniques, artifacts** and models/_methods/artifacts guidance.

**Critical mismatch to understand:** The ECO explicitly states the 2023 taskforce was **NOT bound by the PMBOK Guide** — the CAPM exam goes well beyond PMBOK 7. Every exam question maps to at least two references from this official list:

1. **PMBOK Guide, Seventh Edition** (principles + performance domains)
2. **Process Groups: A Practice Guide (2022)** (restores the predictive process-group mechanics that PMBOK 7 dropped — this is where the "old" process knowledge lives now)
3. **The PMI Guide to Business Analysis (2017)** + **Business Analysis for Practitioners: A Practice Guide, 2nd Ed. (2024)** → covers the entire 27% Business Analysis domain
4. **Agile Practice Guide (2017)** → covers the 20% Agile domain
5. Non-PMI supplements: *The Project Management Answer Book* (2nd Ed.), *Effective Project Management: Traditional, Agile, Extreme, Hybrid* (8th Ed.)

**Dojo implication:** studying PMBOK 7 alone covers roughly the Fundamentals core; the exam adds three whole bodies of content (predictive process mechanics, agile frameworks, business analysis) from companion references.

## 4. Passing Score / Scoring Model

- PMI does **NOT publish a fixed passing score**. The ECO states "the scoring model is periodically reviewed by scoring experts to ensure valid assessment of knowledge and skills" — i.e., determined by **psychometric analysis** (modified Angoff-style standard setting), not a fixed percentage.
- Score reports show pass/fail plus **domain-level performance ratings** (Below Target / Target / Above Target style proficiency levels).
- Community/training-provider consensus estimates ≈ **~70% of scored questions** needed (unofficial; treat as a practice-exam benchmark, not a guarantee).
- Pretest questions (15) do not affect the score. Manual rescore (paper-based administrations only): US$45.

## 5. Eligibility, Cost, Delivery

**Eligibility (no experience required):**
- Secondary degree (high school diploma, GED, or global equivalent), **AND**
- **23 hours of project management education completed before the exam** (via PMI ATPs, PMI chapters, employer programs, training companies, distance learning, or university courses — PMI's own on-demand CAPM Exam Prep Course or instructor-led CAPM course also satisfy it).

**Cost (USD):**

| Item | PMI Member | Non-Member |
|---|---|---|
| Exam fee | **$225** | **$300** |
| Retake (re-exam) fee | $150 | $200 |
| PMI membership (optional, to unlock member price) | ~$139/yr + application fee | — |

Note: membership must be fully processed **before paying** for the exam to get the member rate (no retroactive refunds). Membership also includes free digital copies of PMBOK 7, Process Groups Practice Guide, and Agile Practice Guide — effectively making the member route cheaper overall for a first-time candidate.

**Delivery options:**
- **Pearson VUE test center** (computer-based; full question-type set incl. animation videos with headphones)
- **Online proctored (OPT / OnVUE)** from home or office (comic strips replace animation videos to avoid audio issues)
- 1-year eligibility window; **up to 3 attempts** within it; after 3 failures, wait 1 year to reapply.
- **Recertification:** every 3 years via CCR program (PDUs).

## 6. Typical Question Style + Illustrative STEMS (paraphrased descriptions — not actual copyrighted questions)

CAPM items are predominantly **scenario/application-based**, not pure definition recall. Common patterns:

1. **EVM calculation stem (Predictive):** "A project has [EV/AC/PV given values]. What is the schedule/Cost Performance Index?" → tests whether you can compute and interpret SV/CV/SPI/CPI.
2. **Best-response scenario stem (Agile/Fundamentals):** "A team member on a [Scrum/hybrid] project faces [impediment/change request mid-iteration]. What should the [PM/team member] do FIRST?" → tests "best/most appropriate next action" reasoning with two plausible options.
3. **Matching stem (BA, drag-and-drop format):** Given a scenario listing stakeholders or artifacts, match items (e.g., roles ↔ responsibilities, or artifact ↔ release/phase) across two columns.
4. **Ethics stem (Fundamentals):** "You discover [conflict-of-interest situation]. Per the PMI Code of Ethics, what is the MOST appropriate response?" → values-based judgment.

Distractor design: usually two clearly wrong + two plausible options; the correct answer favors PMI-standard terminology, PMI's order of operations (assess → communicate → escalate), and ethics-first reasoning.

## 7. What This Means for the Dojo (module practice-question weighting)

Recommended question-bank distribution ≈ ECO weights, with coaching emphasis notes:

1. **Fundamentals (36%) — biggest module.** Heaviest question count. Include registers, roles, life cycles, EQ, ethics scenarios, problem-solving tools. Drill "distinguish between X and Y" style (project/portfolio, issue/risk, milestone/duration).
2. **Business Analysis (27%) — the differentiator.** Most candidates under-prepare here (it's newest, 2023). Full module: RTM, backlog, roadmap, acceptance criteria, stakeholder roles, requirements-gathering technique selection.
3. **Agile (20%).** Scrum/XP/Kanban/SAFe comparisons, iteration planning, adaptive artifacts, prioritization. Scenario-heavy.
4. **Predictive (17%).** Smallest weight but most **calculable**: CPM, SV/CV/SPI/CPI, WBS. High value-per-study-hour — build a formula drill mode.
5. **Quiz-app requirements:** include MCQ + drag-drop matching + hotspot-style interactions; timed 150-Q/180-min simulation with a break at Q75 and section lockout; domain-level score reporting (Below Target/Target/Above Target); target ≥75% on mixed timed sets before declaring readiness.
