# CAPM ECO 2023 — Task → Proficiency Objectives (parsed from official PMI ECO PDF)

Source: session/eco_full.txt (official PMI CAPM Exam Content Outline 2023).
Objective IDs: DnTm-k. Rule: every objective needs >=1 lecture section + >=2 quiz questions, scenario-style where the ECO implies scenario testing.


## Domain 1 — Project Management Fundamentals and Core Concepts (36%)

### D1T1. Demonstrate an understanding of the various project life cycles and processes.

- [D1T1-1] Distinguish between a project, program, and a portfolio.
- [D1T1-2] Distinguish between a project and operations.
- [D1T1-3] Distinguish between predictive and adaptive approaches.
- [D1T1-4] Distinguish between issues, risks, assumptions, and constraints.
- [D1T1-5] Review/critique project scope.
- [D1T1-6] Apply the project management code of ethics to scenarios (refer to PMI Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct).
- [D1T1-7] Explain how a project can be a vehicle for change.
### D1T2. Demonstrate an understanding of project management planning.

- [D1T2-1] Describe the purpose and importance of cost, quality, risk, schedule, etc.
- [D1T2-2] Distinguish between the different deliverables of a project management plan versus product management plan.
- [D1T2-3] Distinguish differences between a milestone and a task duration.
- [D1T2-4] Determine the number and type of resources in a project.
- [D1T2-5] Use a risk register in a given situation.
- [D1T2-6] Use a stakeholder register in a given situation.
- [D1T2-7] Explain project closure and transitions.
### D1T3. Demonstrate an understanding of project roles and responsibilities.

- [D1T3-1] Compare and contrast the roles and responsibilities of project managers and project sponsors.
- [D1T3-2] Compare and contrast the roles and responsibilities of the project team and the project sponsor.
- [D1T3-3] Explain the importance of the role the project manager plays (e.g., initiator, negotiator, listener, coach, working member, and facilitator).
- [D1T3-4] Explain the differences between leadership and management.
- [D1T3-5] Explain emotional intelligence (EQ) and its impact on project management.
### D1T4. Determine how to follow and execute planned strategies or frameworks (e.g., communication, risks, etc.).

- [D1T4-1] Give examples of how it is appropriate to respond to a planned strategy or framework (e.g., communication, risk, etc.).
- [D1T4-2] Explain project initiation and benefit planning.
### D1T5. Demonstrate an understanding of common problem-solving tools and techniques.

- [D1T5-1] Evaluate the effectiveness of a meeting.
- [D1T5-2] Explain the purpose of focus groups, standup meetings, brainstorming, etc.

## Domain 2 — Predictive, Plan-Based Methodologies (17%)

### D2T1. Explain when it is appropriate to use a predictive, plan-based approach.

- [D2T1-1] Identify the suitability of a predictive, plan-based approach for the organizational structure (e.g., virtual, colocation, matrix structure, hierarchical, etc.).
- [D2T1-2] Determine the activities within each process.
- [D2T1-3] Give examples of typical activities within each process.
- [D2T1-4] Distinguish the differences between various project components.
### D2T2. Demonstrate an understanding of a project management plan schedule.

- [D2T2-1] Apply critical path methods.
- [D2T2-2] Calculate schedule variance.
- [D2T2-3] Explain work breakdown structures (WBS).
- [D2T2-4] Explain work packages.
- [D2T2-5] Apply a quality management plan.
- [D2T2-6] Apply an integration management plan.
### D2T3. Determine how to document project controls of predictive, plan- based projects.

- [D2T3-1] Identify artifacts that are used in predictive, plan-based projects.
- [D2T3-2] Calculate cost and schedule variances.

## Domain 3 — Agile Frameworks/Methodologies (20%)

### D3T1. Explain when it is appropriate to use an adaptive approach.

- [D3T1-1] Compare the pros and cons of adaptive and predictive, plan-based projects.
- [D3T1-2] Identify the suitability of adaptive approaches for the organizational structure (e.g., virtual, colocation, matrix structure, hierarchical, etc.).
- [D3T1-3] Identify organizational process assets and enterprise environmental factors that facilitate the use of adaptive approaches.
### D3T2. Determine how to plan project iterations.

- [D3T2-1] Distinguish the logical units of iterations.
- [D3T2-2] Interpret the pros and cons of the iteration.
- [D3T2-3] Translate this WBS to an adaptive iteration.
- [D3T2-4] Determine inputs for scope.
- [D3T2-5] Explain the importance of adaptive project tracking versus predictive, plan-based tracking.
### D3T3. Determine how to document project controls for an adaptive project.

- [D3T3-1] Identify artifacts that are used in adaptive projects.
### D3T4. Explain the components of an adaptive plan.

- [D3T4-1] Distinguish between the components of different adaptive methodologies (e.g., Scrum, Extreme Programming (XP), Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe®), Kanban, etc.).
### D3T5. Determine how to prepare and execute task management steps.

- [D3T5-1] Interpret success criteria of an adaptive project management task.
- [D3T5-2] Prioritize tasks in adaptive project management.

## Domain 4 — Business Analysis Frameworks (27%)

### D4T1. Demonstrate an understanding of business analysis (BA) roles and responsibilities.

- [D4T1-1] Distinguish between stakeholder roles (e.g., process owner, process manager, product manager, product owner, etc.).
- [D4T1-2] Outline the need for roles and responsibilities (Why do you need to identify stakeholders in the first place?).
- [D4T1-3] Differentiate between internal and external roles.
### D4T2. Determine how to conduct stakeholder communication.

- [D4T2-1] Recommend the most appropriate communication channel/tool (e.g., reporting, presentation, etc.).
- [D4T2-2] Demonstrate why communication is important for a business analyst between various teams (features, requirements, etc.).
### D4T3. Determine how to gather requirements.

- [D4T3-1] Match tools to scenarios (e.g., user stories, use cases, etc.).
- [D4T3-2] Identify the requirements gathering approach for a situation (e.g., conduct stakeholder interviews, surveys, workshops, lessons learned, etc.).
- [D4T3-3] Explain a requirements traceability matrix/product backlog.
### D4T4. Demonstrate an understanding of product roadmaps.

- [D4T4-1] Explain the application of a product roadmap.
- [D4T4-2] Determine which components go to which releases.
### D4T5. Determine how project methodologies influence business analysis processes.

- [D4T5-1] Determine the role of a business analyst in adaptive and/or predictive, plan-based approaches.
### D4T6. Validate requirements through product delivery.

- [D4T6-1] Define acceptance criteria (the action of defining changes based on the situation).
- [D4T6-2] Determine if a project/product is ready for delivery based on a requirements traceability matrix/product backlog.
